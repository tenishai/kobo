# Personas
<!-- summary: Built-in personas, building your own, the naming rule, and which group a persona belongs to. -->

A persona is the soul of an agent: who it is, how it works, and its always and never rules. An agent is built from exactly one. The **Personas** page in the left rail lists them all.

## Built-in personas

The room ships a set of general personas for common kinds of agent, kept to the house standard: efficient, pleasant, plain. They sit first in every list and belong to everyone. Among them are a code reviewer, an onboarding guide, a billing analyst, a legal reader, a frontend designer, a marketing strategist, an identity admin, a workspace admin, and steady operators for each major cloud. Start from one of these whenever it fits.

## Personas from your groups

Below the built-ins are personas people have made. On the create form you see your groups' personas by default; **Show all groups** shows everyone's. A persona is filed under the group that made it, so it stays that team's unless they choose to share it.

## Build your own

From the create form, **Build your own persona** opens the builder. Two ways in:

- **Paste their persona.** You already have a persona written. Paste it or upload the file, and the room reads it into the fields for you.
- **Describe the job.** A sentence or two about what the agent should do, and the room drafts a persona you then edit.

Either way you land on one form: a name, one line for who they are, what they do, optional rules one per line, and **Who is this persona for**, which is the group it is filed under. Press **Create persona** and it is pickable at once.

## The naming rule

A persona name is one or two words, three at most, like `sentinel`, `ledger-keeper` or `billing-analyst`. The room refuses a longer one. Traits belong in the who-they-are line, not in the name. Names are lowercase letters, numbers and hyphens.

## Moving and archiving

On the Personas page, a persona's maker, a member of its group, or an operator can **Move to** another group or to everyone. **Archive** takes it out of every list without deleting it; agents already built from it keep running, and **Show archived** brings it back. Built-in personas stay shared and never move.
